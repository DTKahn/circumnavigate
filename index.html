        What is a great circle?

        • The biggest circle on a ball. Slice an orange straight through its middle and the cut edge is a great circle. Cut anywhere else and you get a smaller circle.
        • Always the same length. Every great circle on Earth is about 40,030 km long, no matter which way it points.
        • The shortest way between two places. That’s why long flights seem to curve on flat maps: pilots follow great circles.
        • Halves the world. A great circle splits Earth into two equal hemispheres, and always passes through the exact opposite side of the planet from where it starts, called the antipode.
        • Famous examples. The Equator and every line of longitude are great circles. Other lines of latitude are not.
